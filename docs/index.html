Questions & answers.

Will this get my account banned?

Automating X can violate its Terms of Service, so there is always some risk. x-unfollow is deliberately slow - 20 to 50 seconds between each unfollow, with a longer break every ten - to stay well under the radar, but no tool can promise safety. Go at a modest pace and use it at your own risk.

Does it need my password or an API key?

No. It runs inside your own logged-in browser session. There is no login form, no API key, and nothing is ever sent to a server - everything happens on your machine.

How fast does it go, and is there a limit?

It paces itself at 20 to 50 seconds per unfollow, with a longer rest every ten, and stops at a hard ceiling of 600 a day. The daily count is remembered even if you close the tab.

Can I keep accounts I do not want to lose?

Yes. Add any handles to the protected list in the popup and they are skipped every time, no matter how large the run.

Can I close the popup or switch tabs?

You can close the popup - the engine runs in the page and keeps going. Keep the tab open, though. If you switch to another tab, Chrome slows the timers down (never up), so it simply runs a little slower.

Is it really free, and which browsers work?

Completely free and open source under the MIT license - no account, no upsell. It works in any Chromium browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge) loaded through Developer mode.
