x-unfollow

Bulk-unfollow on X at a human pace, from your own browser. Free and open source. No API keys, no passwords, nothing leaves your machine.

Download ZIP View on GitHub

Why it is safe to run

  • Runs entirely inside your own logged-in browser. Your session and cookies never leave your machine.
  • Randomized 20-50 second delay between unfollows, plus a 4-10 minute break every 10.
  • Hard cap of 600 unfollows per day. The counter survives restarts.
  • A protected-handles list it will never unfollow. You edit it in the popup.
  • Unknown popups are dismissed, never confirmed. Only the unfollow confirmation step clicks confirm.
  • Open source and dependency-light. Read every line before you run it.

Install (about 2 minutes)

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder from the unzipped download.
  5. Log in to X and open x.com/yourhandle/following.
  6. Click the x-unfollow icon, set a session size, press Start.

Prefer a terminal?

The same engine ships as a small Node.js CLI in the repo. See the README.

Automating actions on X may violate the X Terms of Service and can lead to rate limits or account restrictions. This tool is intentionally slow and conservative, but no guarantees are made. Use at your own risk. It can only unfollow from your own following list; it cannot post, reply, like, or follow.
